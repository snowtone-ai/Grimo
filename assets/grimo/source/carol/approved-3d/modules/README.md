## Authority boundary

These are **localized module construction helpers**, not whole-character canonical references.

Authority remains:

```text
Carol canonical identity
> approved Normal Front / Normal Side
> current full-spatial Hero appearance
> exposed-Hero module helper sheets in their local domains
> approved Skin Front / Skin Side + CAROL_GEOMETRY_PARAMETERS.md
> implementation convenience
```

The module sheets must not be used to redesign Carol's whole-character proportions, face, fleece, support spacing, or camera.

The 3/4 panel is a validation view, not an independent fitting authority.

The cream attachment/root or pastern stubs are **orientation-only helpers**. Do not copy their visible cut-cylinder boundary into final Carol geometry.

Numerical construction locks and tolerances live in:
`docs/production/carol/CAROL_GEOMETRY_PARAMETERS.md`.

## Fleece relationship

These modules are intended to remain visible and readable after fleece integration. Fleece may overlap attachment regions naturally, but it must not erase the approved ear/hoof identity, create clipping, or force view-specific geometry.
