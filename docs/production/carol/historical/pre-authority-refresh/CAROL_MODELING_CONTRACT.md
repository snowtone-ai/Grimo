# Carol modeling contract

## Identity and evidence

Current mutable candidate, Human Gate, blockers, and next step live only in
[`CAROL_PRODUCTION_STATE.md`](CAROL_PRODUCTION_STATE.md). This contract keeps
stable modeling rules; it must not duplicate current status.

Final-use authority order:

1. `assets/grimo/source/carol/carol-Identity-canonical.png` — highest visible
   identity / cuteness / appeal authority.
2. Approved Normal Front / Normal Side — locked visible neutral references.
3. Full-spatial fleece-included Carol realization.
4. HERO_PRIORITY / MOTION_CRITICAL exterior geometry.
5. Skin Front / Skin Side +
   `docs/production/carol/CAROL_GEOMETRY_PARAMETERS.md` — supporting underbody
   authority for support, rigging, deformation, attachment, clearance, and
   spatial continuity.
6. FUNCTIONAL_HIDDEN implementation.

The four locked production images must not be regenerated. Older six-view
references and historical geometry may be used as donor/benchmark evidence but
do not override current authority. Back/Top/3Q are derived diagnostics.

One coherent full-spatial Carol is required, but one continuous watertight Hero
body is not. Modular/separate meshes are valid when they behave as one character
through practical views and motion. Do not create separate view-specific Carol
characters or camera-dependent neutral geometry.

Do not substitute generic sheep anatomy for Carol. Absence of a measurement is
an unresolved question, not permission to invent a new visual identity.

## Production boundaries

- Baseline: **Full-Spatial 3D Living Character Architecture with View-Weighted
  Polish**.
- Exterior geometry must remain coherent through practical Front / 3/4 / Side /
  Rear / derived Top exposure and plausible future motion.
- Front Hero presentation receives the highest identity/appeal polish; this does
  not permit off-axis structural collapse.
- Geometry classes are **HERO_PRIORITY**, **GENERAL_EXTERIOR**,
  **MOTION_CRITICAL**, and **FUNCTIONAL_HIDDEN**.
- Modular/separate meshes are allowed. One watertight body is not required.
- Visible Human-facing probes must meet the declared Human-Evaluable Fidelity
  Floor. A dominant off-model proxy makes the probe PROBE_INVALID.
- Existing stronger geometry/fleece assets are reuse-first donors before a
  lower-quality replacement is generated.
- Carol fleece is a full-spatial Hero identity system. It must have real volume,
  coherent Front/Side/Rear/derived-Top form, and stable ownership around
  face/ears/limbs/tail.
- Naked Underbody Hero beauty is not an independent gate. Its job is support,
  deformation, attachment, clearance, and spatial continuity.
- Hero rendering should respect `CAROL_CAMERA_CONTRACT.md`; camera framing does
  not define which geometry is allowed to exist.
- Physics must not decide Hero acting, facial meaning, touch reaction, main body
  acting, or primary fleece silhouette.
- Topology/intersection checks are diagnostic evidence. A defect blocks when it
  creates or credibly threatens visible full-spatial failure, deformation,
  attachment, interaction, export/runtime failure, or future-motion rework.
- Final visible acceptance remains Human authority.

## Motion-readiness boundary

Keep neutral geometry compatible with required support and independent
head/eyes/ears/forelimbs/tail/fleece controls, contact-seeking motion, and
interruption.

The model should not require structural rebuilding merely because a later
approved motion exposes an additional practical angle.

Production topology, final weights, final materials, and the complete motion
library may remain provisional while representative behavior is tested, but
visible components used for Human judgment must already be representative enough
for that judgment.

Do not pre-authorize rigging, animation, or another geometry repair from this
contract alone; the Decision Question and attempt limit come from the Production
Operating System and `CAROL_PRODUCTION_STATE.md`.
