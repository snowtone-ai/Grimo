# 4. Eye / socket correction

## 4.1 Front identity — CURRENT REPO LOCK

Do NOT solve the Side/3Q problem by shrinking the Front eye.

Per eye:
- visible Front width = `0.137 H`
- visible Front height = `0.149 H`
- center-to-center spacing = `0.324 H`
- eye centers = `Y ~= +/-0.162 H` from face centerline

The huge-eye identity is intentional and must remain.

## 4.2 Current defect

Human review of the v002 integrated Side/3Q view:
- the eye reads as protruding / bulging out of the head ("出目")
- the defect is strongest away from strict Front
- this is a socket seating / eye-cap depth / lid coverage problem, not permission to reduce the approved Front eye aperture

Historical evidence also recorded a protruding Side eye on the retained v011 family.

## 4.3 Eye depth correction — NEW NEXT-REVISION TARGET

The historical selected eye prototype had approximately `0.029 H` central relief.
For the next revision:

- target visible central cap relief beyond the local socket envelope: `0.010 H .. 0.015 H`
- hard upper limit for obvious local protrusion beyond surrounding socket/lid envelope: `0.016 H`
- target Side visible forward bulge relative to the local facial/socket silhouette: `<= 0.010 H`

These are correction targets, not permanent identity locks.

Primary strategy:
1. preserve Front aperture width/height/centers
2. seat the eye body deeper into the cranial socket
3. increase socket/lid wrap around the peripheral eye
4. reduce exposed spherical/ellipsoidal sidewall
5. keep only a shallow soft convex corneal/eye-cap read
6. preserve the glossy large-eye identity from Front

Do NOT:
- shrink the approved Front eye
- flatten the eye into a card
- hide the eye so much that Front identity changes
- create camera-specific eye geometry
- move the camera to disguise protrusion

## 4.4 3Q / Side acceptance criteria — NEW NEXT-REVISION TARGET

Human-visible requirements:
- eye must feel embedded in the face, not glued on top
- no bead / marble / hemisphere protrusion
- visible sidewall of the eyeball should be minimal
- lid/socket rim must visually own the eye perimeter
- 3Q eye must retain cute oval identity without becoming horizontally stretched
- Side eye should remain readable but subordinate to the head silhouette

Quantitative diagnostics:
- Front aperture dimensions and centers remain within existing contract tolerance
- no eye/head mesh interpenetration blocker
- no detached eyelid gap
- compare 3Q/Side eye silhouette and protrusion against approved Normal/Skin Side + canonical identity
