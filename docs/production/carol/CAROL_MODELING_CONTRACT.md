# Carol modeling contract

## Identity and evidence

Current mutable candidate, Human Gate, blockers, and next step live only in
[`CAROL_PRODUCTION_STATE.md`](CAROL_PRODUCTION_STATE.md). This contract keeps
stable modeling rules; it must not duplicate current status.

Final-use authority order:

1. `assets/grimo/source/carol/carol-Identity-canonical.png` — highest visible
   identity / cuteness / appeal authority.
2. Approved Normal Front / Normal Side — locked visible neutral references.
3. Fleece-included Hero appearance in the actual interaction envelope.
4. MOTION_EXPOSED geometry.
5. Skin Front / Skin Side +
   `docs/production/carol/CAROL_GEOMETRY_PARAMETERS.md` — supporting underbody
   authority for support, rigging, deformation, attachment, and clearance.
6. FUNCTIONAL_HIDDEN implementation.

The four locked production images must not be regenerated. Older six-view
references and v006 geometry are historical. Back/Top/3Q are derived diagnostics.

One coherent spatial Carol is required, but one continuous watertight Hero body
is not. Modular/separate meshes are valid when required seams, deformation,
attachment, clearance, export, and motion behavior remain acceptable. Do not
create separate view-specific Carol characters.

Do not substitute generic sheep anatomy for Carol. Absence of a measurement is
an unresolved question, not permission to invent a new visual identity.

## Production boundaries

- Baseline: Front-Optimized 3D-First Living Character Architecture.
  Blender-centered authoring and GLB/glTF → PlayCanvas are implementation
  hypotheses subordinate to the Product North Star.
- The front-facing Hero envelope gets priority; equal-fidelity 360° beauty is
  not required.
- Classify geometry as **HERO_VISIBLE**, **MOTION_EXPOSED**, or
  **FUNCTIONAL_HIDDEN** and invest accordingly.
- Static intermediate perfection is not prerequisite for provisional fleece,
  rig, representative motion, GLB/runtime, or device probes when those are the
  cheapest falsifiable way to answer a material unknown.
- Carol fleece is a Hero identity system. Naked Underbody visual perfection is
  not an independent gate before fleece/motion/runtime.
- Hero rendering should respect `CAROL_CAMERA_CONTRACT.md`; approved motion may
  expose transition/off-axis surfaces.
- Physics must not decide Hero acting, facial meaning, touch reaction, main body
  acting, or primary fleece silhouette.
- Topology/intersection/geometry checks are diagnostic evidence. A defect blocks
  when it creates or credibly threatens visible, deformation, attachment,
  interaction, export, or runtime failure. If impact is unknown, probe final
  use first.
- Final visible acceptance remains Human authority.

## Motion-readiness boundary

Keep neutral geometry compatible with required support and independent
head/eyes/ears/forelimbs/tail/fleece controls, contact-seeking motion, and
interruption. Do not pre-authorize rigging, animation, or another geometry
repair from this contract alone; the Decision Question and attempt limit come
from the Production Operating System and `CAROL_PRODUCTION_STATE.md`.
