Agent inspected actual canonical/approved reference pixels, the direct historical

## Loop 1 — identity recovery

**Observed before:** v003 has centered generic face, horizontal eyes, nearly
mirrored ears, a dark hood-like opening and oversized smooth rear oval.

**Cause:** generic visible-part geometry and loss of authored source relationships;
prior global smoothing removed medium fleece forms.

**Changed:** mapped BODY/FACE/HEAD/ear traces through normalized source space;
rebuilt closed face/ears, source-relative eye axis and facial assembly; evaluated
original BODY/HEAD fifth-norm relief on a full-depth closed envelope. Preserved
the chassis, four supports/hooves, tuft and cameras.

**Expected:** recover the angled face/ear identity without sacrificing depth.

**Actual:** angled face, different left/right ears and eye placement are plainly
closer to source. However, radial contour influence continued to the poles,
making spokes on the rear; the new lower envelope exposed support shafts and
chassis. Face had weak fleece contact; front valleys were too dimple-like.
Blender front/side/back were viewed. An initial browser capture had viewport
clipping; it was rejected and the comparison viewport was corrected, without
changing the model camera.

## Loop 2 — fleece field and contact recovery

**Observed:** radial rear spokes, exposed chassis, face separated from fleece,
and insufficient directional side relief in Loop 1.

**Cause:** using the exact angular outline at every radial latitude creates
fluting; the source illustration's bottom contour does not wrap the preserved
neutral support roots; one body depth field under-serves the source head region.

**Changed:** fade the detailed trace into an ellipse toward front/rear centers;
union a closed HEAD-trace envelope and low support cover into the continuous
fleece; strengthen selected side/back banks. Reduced the nose/mouth/cheek relief.

**Expected:** remove spokes and chassis exposure, improve face embedding and
side hierarchy.

**Actual:** rear spokes disappeared and chassis became concealed. Full depth,
tuft and support separation survived. Face and asymmetric ears remained legible.
Side/back still had broad smooth areas; lower cover read as a horizontal band;
forehead did not sufficiently overlap the face. Front/side/back renders viewed.

## Loop 3 — Full-3D continuity and final correction

**Observed:** inadequate forehead contact, broad side/rear banks, exposed shaft
tops, and a visibly corrugated moon in Loop 2.

**Cause:** head front depth insufficient at the upper FACE boundary; side field
centers leave large regions without a medium ridge; low cover is too high;
concave motif tessellation across steep fleece changes creates ribbon artifacts.

**Changed:** move the head envelope forward while retaining broad shallow
superellipse recession; reduce/shallow and slightly broaden front lock relief;
add specified medium oblique side/back controls; lower/widen the cover; replace
moon ngon with a closed cross-strip whose attachment depth is smoothed.

**Expected:** more natural embedding and medium relief, concealed root shafts,
clean moon, full-depth structure retained.

**Actual:** forehead/cheek overlap improved; dark deep v003 socket did not return;
moon corrugation removed; central rear oval replaced by sweeping unequal regions.
Side/back depth and three visible supports at 3/4 confirm a volumetric body.
Nevertheless, the side is still too pillow-like, front valleys are repetitive,
lower band is conspicuous, eyes have raised button-like construction in clay,
left ear root is too exposed, and mouth is still simplified. No fourth geometry
loop. Agent supporting result: **FAIL for full canonical-quality convergence**.
Human result remains **PENDING**; the agent does not choose the Human result.

## Historical viewer corrections (not geometry loops)

The supplied 1.65/transparent lighting recipe was not found at the verified HEAD.
The direct page uses exposure 1.15/opaque stage, different lights and preserved
unlit paint with tone mapping disabled. Both environments are labeled.
Initial reconstruction left saved morph influences active and tone-mapped the
old paint, producing distorted partial eyes and washed colors. Those diagnostic
captures were superseded: set historical morph influences to zero and preserve
the page's material handling. This did not change the historical GLB. The final
neutral comparison matches the direct page's painted eye/face relationships.
