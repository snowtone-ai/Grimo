## 2. Carol Motion North Star

Carol is **attention-seeking + relaxed**.

```text
notice softly
→ make a small spatial offer
→ wait
→ acknowledge contact immediately and locally
→ let face/head carry meaning
→ commit only necessary body mass
→ let fleece echo later
→ remain warm after the action
```

Non-negotiable signature:
- face / eyelid / head lead;
- touch first reads at local cheek/fleece;
- low–medium energy, usually small amplitude;
- soft onset but fast local ACK;
- heavy grounded hooves;
- subtle asymmetric ears;
- long soft gaze dwell + natural release;
- high tolerance for intentional stillness;
- broad fleece moves after meaning is readable;
- rare medium emotional peak;
- long warm afterglow.

Reject: energetic Eevee clone, Pikachu paw-chain identity, bouncing cloud ball, jelly fleece, constant ear motion.

## 4. Motion Coverage Model

Three layers:

- **Layer 1 — 34 Built-in Life Families:** complete companion without unlocks.
- **Layer 2 — 8 Built-in Richness Families:** low-frequency/context-dependent discoveries available from first launch.
- **Layer 3 — 4 Unlockable Motion Families + 2 Special items:** new play/rare events.

The following **18 modular primitives** are not counted as Motion Families: neutral blink; blink suppression/release; eyelid soften/half-lid; gaze notice; gaze dwell; gaze release; tiny head correction; one-ear attention L/R; ear relax L/R; tiny breath/posture; grounded micro weight shift; hoof micro-adjust component; local cheek compression; local fleece compression; local fleece settle; broader fleece settle; small mouth/content-smile; short afterglow facial residue.

They are selected/masked by state/history; never all run at once.

## 9. Social Initiative / WAIT

Normal invitation:
```text
socialSeeking → orient → cheek/forehead/fleece-opening presentation → WAIT → accepted/ignored
```

Presentation variants: cheek L, cheek R, forehead lower, face-in-fleece opening.

WAIT allows blink/tiny breath/one-ear monitor, but no guilt. Ignored: one hopeful glance at most, then soft release.

Accepted contact gets fast local/social ACK, then either one second-turn offer or completion. The low-forehoof ritual is rare Layer 2 and never a standard paw chain.

## 10. Boundary / Withdrawal

```text
HINT → REFUSAL → WITHDRAWAL + RESIDUE → RECOVERY
```

Hysteresis prevents instant happy flip. No aggression, slap/kick/bite, electric anger, guilt or punitive sadness.

## 14. Secret Motion Library

### Moon Ball Play
`BOTH`; prop `moon_ball`. Eyes/head notice → cheek/forehead nudge → WAIT for user tap/flick → optional second turn → cuddle-like settle. Cooldown 60–120s. HIGH front readability. Needs gaze target, prop trajectory, contact marker, safe head/neck and local fleece.

### Star Peek
`BOTH`; prop `dream_star`. Star peeks from L/R/front fleece pockets; Carol tracks it; user taps; wrong/late input gets gentle re-present. Cooldown 75–150s. Needs front-fleece pocket markers and side-conditioned gaze.

### Fleece Hide-and-Peek
`BOTH`; no external prop. Carol sinks face into fleece; user taps visible opening/cheek; one side swap max; returns via local corrective deformation. Cooldown 90–180s. Needs face-in-fleece compression, semantic touch anchors and silhouette clamps.

Each is interactive; none is “one long clip.”

## 19. Repetition Suppression / History

Starting history buffer = last **8** meaningful selections.

Store: `motionFamilyId`, variant, similarityGroup, contactSide, zone, gesture, emotionOutcome, lastUsedAt, recentUseCount.

Rules:
- no consecutive exact full-chain repeat for major touch;
- strong similarity penalty if previous two reactions share the same silhouette group;
- rare/hero uses hard cooldown;
- L/R may not collapse to centered macro response;
- preferred slow-pet ×10 must vary intensity/side/face/ear/settle/afterglow while preserving affection semantics;
- repetition can reduce intensity rather than always escalating.

## 20. Interruption Rules

Priority:
```text
identity/safety > local touch ACK > active interaction > reciprocal social turn
> protected hero/secret peak > attention/face continuity > support
> autonomous > physiology > cosmetic residual/VFX
```

Touch during idle overlays local ACK; WAIT accepts offered-target touch; side change transfers through settle; touch release branches to intensity-matched settle; afterglow is interruptible from its current pose; feed bite/prop-transfer windows use authored safe nodes; background does not simulate realtime animation—resume reconciles elapsed state.

## 21. Front-View Readability

**Human presentation clarification — 2026-09-16:** the front-facing camera is
the reference, not a lock on Carol's orientation. Future Full Companion shows
the whole low quadruped and allows turns, three-quarter/back views, rotation
and walking. Future Close Window-Lean means approach, rear weight shift,
forebody lift and front hooves near/on the bottom viewport boundary. These
states are conceptual; v002 implements no runtime states or motion and does
not change the adopted Family counts or approve a Close clip.

Every adopted MVP Family is designed **HIGH** from the front interaction camera.

No core interaction relies on back/tail visibility. Partial lateral/depth motion must have readable face/ear/fleece silhouette cues. Camera orbit is forbidden as a fix for weak acting.

## 33. Final One-Page Carol Motion Doctrine

Carol is not “a sheep model with animations.” She is a low, grounded, attention-seeking but relaxed companion whose tiny face lives inside a huge dream-cloud fleece.

**Life**
```text
state is always alive ≠ every channel is always moving
```
Quiet is valid. Blink, gaze, one ear, tiny head correction, support adjustment and fleece residual operate at different cadences. Fixed oscillators do not create life.

**Touch**
```text
contacted zone → local ACK → face/attention → head commitment
→ only necessary torso/COM → fleece echo → settle → warm residue
```
Contact side survives. Continuous touch accumulates; it does not retrigger a clip.

**Personality**
Small movement beats large movement. Soft onset never means slow causality. Hooves are heavy. Ears are subtle. Fleece is delayed, not gelatinous. Carol can want affection before the user touches her.

**Social agency**
Carol offers cheek, forehead or a fleece opening and waits. The rare forehoof ritual stays exceptional.

**Boundary**
`hint → refusal → withdrawal/residue → re-engagement`, with no hostility.

**Food**
`notice → consume/refuse → evaluate → preference 1–5 → afterglow`.

**Built-in richness**
Dream holds, deeper cheek press, face-in-fleece peek, delayed affection, motif recognition and a grounded hero delight are already present before unlocks.

**Unlockables**
Special = Carol-specific Food/Wear collectible. Secret Motion = genuinely new play. Gift = Carol initiates and physically presents something.

**Front view**
All adopted MVP Families must read from the normal front companion camera. Camera motion may not rescue weak acting.
