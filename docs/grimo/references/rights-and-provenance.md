# Rights and Provenance Rules

## Grimo source/reference images

- The four Grimo canonical images are the product identity authority.
- The 32 generated images are implementation references, not new canonical designs.
- If a reference image conflicts with canonical, canonical wins.
- Preserve provenance in the repo; do not silently promote a generated helper image to canonical.

## Pokémon / third-party references

This import pack intentionally does **not** contain Pokémon videos, screenshots, extracted frames, audio, icons, card art, or other third-party media.

Repository-safe material here is limited to:
- URLs and channel/source names
- user-provided timestamps/observations
- derived interaction/design principles
- notes about candidate references that still need viewing/annotation

Do not download and commit third-party YouTube videos or Pokémon TCG Pocket screenshots as project assets unless the user separately decides to manage those rights outside the public repository.
