## Carol anatomy mapping

- Head/face: upper mesh region. Eevee tilt direction, dip/lift order, and hold duration map to authored head X/Y/rotation values. The canonical Carol face pixels are unchanged.
- Gaze: central face-region shear at much smaller travel than the head. Gaze can lead the head; closed-eye states are represented by posture/track metadata because the accepted flat canonical currently has no eyelid layer.
- Ears: left/right top-corner regions receive independent authored rotations. Asymmetric phases are retained.
- Root/body: central fleece/body region receives authored lateral shift and compression. There is no continuous scale oscillation.
- Hooves: bottom 22% of the source is multiplied by a grounding falloff so authored travel reaches zero at the hoof baseline.
- Fleece: an explicit, later keyframe track follows the primary head/root action. It borrows the source secondary timing relationship, not Eevee's tail shape.
- Paw/item deviations: Carol's accepted canonical is currently one flat source mesh, so a raised paw or item reveal cannot be isolated without inventing/duplicating pixels. The partner-invite and present motions preserve timing, gaze, forward weight, hold, and release but omit prop/limb choreography. This is an anatomical/technical limitation and is scored accordingly.
